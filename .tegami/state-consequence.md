### `voice/state-consequence`: a condition and the consequence make a whole sentence

A guideline the judge weighs: `When worktrees are enabled, each session works in a worktree`, not `Enabled, a session works in a worktree` or `Offline, the app queues each change`. `voice/fronted-modifier` finds the participles a pattern can; the judge finds a state written any other way.
