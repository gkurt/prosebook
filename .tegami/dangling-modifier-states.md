### `voice/dangling-modifier` finds only a setting's state opening a sentence

`Enabled,`, `Left empty,`, `Set to {mode},`, `Off,` and `Based on…,` are still found. A participle describing the subject after the comma (`Opened from the Finder, the app…`), a verb with the verb's object (`Added {name},`), and a list or a noun phrase opening with an adjective pass.
